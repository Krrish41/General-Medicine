# General Medicine Master Study Suite

A comprehensive, interactive 15-mark university examination monograph and clinical preparation platform for BDS (3rd Year) and MBBS General Medicine examinations.

## Core Features
* **15-Mark Essay Models**: All 20 clinical conditions structured according to the 10-tier university examination standard.
* **Master PDF Monograph**: 110-page print-ready, copyable, and searchable PDF (`General_Medicine_Master_Notes.pdf`).
* **Interactive Dashboard**: Searchable, responsive web application (`index.html`) with topic filtering, differential diagnosis tables, and viva pearls.
* **Dental Considerations**: Comprehensive coverage of AHA antibiotic prophylaxis for infective endocarditis, safe bleeding and platelet cutoffs, chairside emergency protocols (hypoglycemia, angina, syncope), and oral manifestations.

## Curriculum Contents
1. **Acute Viral Hepatitis & Chronic Hepatitis** (HAV, HBV, HCV, HDV, HEV, Dane particle, serology, DAAs)
2. **Acute Liver Failure & Hepatic Encephalopathy** (West Haven criteria, Lactulose, Rifaximin, cerebral edema)
3. **Cirrhosis & Portal Hypertension** (Child-Pugh, MELD, SAAG, esophageal varices, EVBL, ascites)
4. **Protein Energy Malnutrition** (Kwashiorkor vs Marasmus, edema pathophysiology, WHO 3-phase stabilization)
5. **Dysphagia & Peptic Ulcer Disease** (Mechanical vs Motor, Plummer-Vinson, H. pylori, Forrest classification)
6. **Acute Rheumatic Fever & RHD** (Revised Jones criteria, Aschoff nodules, penicillin secondary prophylaxis)
7. **Infective Endocarditis & AHA Dental Prophylaxis** (Modified Duke criteria, S. viridans, AHA 2021 dental guidelines)
8. **Ischemic Heart Disease & STEMI** (ECG localization, Troponins, MONA-B protocol, Primary PCI vs Fibrinolysis)
9. **Heart Failure & Systemic Hypertension** (Preload/Afterload, NYHA, GDMT 4 pillars, JNC-7/8, CCB gingival hyperplasia)
10. **Congenital Heart Diseases** (ASD, VSD, PDA, Tetralogy of Fallot, hypercyanotic spells, knee-chest position)
11. **Enteric Fever (Typhoid & Paratyphoid)** (Salmonella typhi, Faget sign, rose spots, BASU schedule, carrier state)
12. **Syphilis & Sexually Transmitted Diseases** (Primary chancre, Secondary snail-track ulcers, Tertiary gumma, Hutchinson triad)
13. **Systemic Bacterial Infections: Diphtheria & Tetanus** (C. diphtheriae pseudomembrane; C. tetani tetanospasmin, trismus)
14. **Systemic Viral Exanthems & HIV/AIDS** (Measles Koplik spots, Rubella, Mumps, HIV CD4 stages, Oral Hairy Leukoplakia, Kaposi sarcoma)
15. **Diabetes Mellitus & Diabetic Ketoacidosis** (ADA diagnostic criteria, DKA management, periodontitis, chairside hypoglycemia)
16. **The Leukemias** (AML, ALL, CML, CLL, Philadelphia BCR-ABL, leukemic gingival hyperplasia, dental hematological cutoffs)
17. **Nephrotic vs Nephritic Syndrome & CKD** (Proteinuria >3.5g/d, eGFR staging, uremic stomatitis, hemodialysis dental protocol)
18. **Facial Nerve Paralysis (Bell's Palsy) & Headaches** (UMN vs LMN facial palsy, Bell's phenomenon, steroids, trigeminal neuralgia)
19. **Acute Meningitis & Neurological Infections** (Pyogenic vs Viral vs TB CSF analysis, Kernig/Brudzinski signs, Ceftriaxone)
20. **Shock Syndromes & CPR** (Hypovolemic, Cardiogenic, Septic, Anaphylactic IM adrenaline, BLS/ACLS 30:2 CPR algorithms)
