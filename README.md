## 15-Mark University Examination Structure

Every medical topic in this monograph strictly adheres to the university 15-mark essay blueprint:
1. **Definition, Classification & Epidemiology**
2. **Etiopathogenesis & Pathophysiology** (molecular, cellular, and hemodynamic cascades)
3. **Clinical Presentation & Bedside Signs** (Inspection, Palpation, Percussion, Auscultation, Named Signs)
4. **Diagnostic Criteria & Clinical Scoring** (Duke, Jones, Child-Pugh, MELD, West Haven, ADA, CURB-65)
5. **Structured Differential Diagnosis** (comprehensive comparative matrices)
6. **Diagnostic Workup** (Routine hemogram, serology, ECG/Echo, imaging: USG/CT/MRI, Endoscopy, Histopathology)
7. **Comprehensive Evidence-Based Management**:
   - Resuscitation & Emergency Stabilization (ABCs, IV fluids, inotropes, emergency doses)
   - Definitive Pharmacotherapy (First-line drug classes, mechanisms, exact doses, side effects)
   - Procedural & Interventional Care (Paracentesis, Dialysis, EVBL, Pacemaker)
   - Complication protocols
8. **Dental & Maxillofacial Considerations** (AHA antibiotic prophylaxis, bleeding/platelet cutoffs, chairside emergency management)
9. **High-Yield Examiner Viva Pearls**
10. **Must-Draw Algorithms, Flowcharts & Schematics**

## Dental Chairside Emergency & Surgical Cutoffs

- **Infective Endocarditis Prophylaxis**: Amoxicillin 2g PO 30–60 min prior to procedure (or Clindamycin 600mg / Azithromycin 500mg if penicillin-allergic).
- **Platelet Count for Extractions**: $> 50,000/\mu\text{L}$ for minor extractions; $> 80,000/\mu\text{L}$ for major surgery.
- **PT / INR Safe Range**: $\text{INR} \le 2.5 - 3.0$ for routine minor oral surgery without stopping warfarin.
- **Fasting Blood Glucose**: $< 180\text{ mg/dL}$ for elective dental procedures; chairside oral glucose (15–20g) for hypoglycemia.
- **Severe Hypertension Alert**: Systolic $> 180\text{ mmHg}$ or Diastolic $> 110\text{ mmHg}$ requires deferral of elective treatment and immediate medical referral.
